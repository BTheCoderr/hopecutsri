# Hope Cuts RI — Brand Identity & Website Design Strategy

A comprehensive brand and design guide for Hope Cuts RI, a Rhode Island-based non-profit offering haircuts as a catalyst for connection, mental health support, autism awareness, homelessness outreach, and substance abuse recovery.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Color Palette

### Primary Palette (Barber Pole Heritage)

| Color | Hex | Usage | Notes |
|-------|-----|--------|------|
| **Hope Red** | `#C41E3A` | CTAs, accents, headings | Classic barber pole red; warm and bold |
| **Patriot Blue** | `#1E3A8A` | Headings, trust elements, footer | Deep, calming blue (not electric) |
| **Clean White** | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, spacious layouts | Creates breathing room; lets logo pop |

### Calming Neutrals (Non-Profit Balance)

| Color | Hex | Usage | Notes |
|-------|-----|--------|------|
| **Slate 900** | `#0F172A` | Body text, dark sections | Professional, readable |
| **Slate 600** | `#475569` | Secondary text, captions | Softer than black |
| **Slate 200** | `#E2E8F0` | Dividers, borders, subtle backgrounds | Soft, not cold |
| **Warm Gray** | `#F8FAFC` | Section backgrounds | Slightly warmer than pure white |

### Accent (Optional Warmth)

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|--------|
| **Hope Gold** | `#D4A853` | Sparingly—badges, "Donate" emphasis, impact highlights |

### Color Philosophy

- **60%** Neutral (white, slate grays) — calm, professional, non-commercial
- **25%** Slate/charcoal — grounding and credibility
- **15%** Red & Blue — patriotic, bold, emotionally resonant

The goal: feel like a **trusted community organization** first, not a barbershop. The red and blue should feel supportive, not overwhelming.

## 3. Modern UI Design Concept: Clean & Bold

### Core Principle: **Flat + Spacious**

The logo is busy and 3D. The website must be the opposite:

- **Flat** — no gradients or heavy shadows on UI elements
- **Generous white space** — sections breathe; content is scannable
- **Clear hierarchy** — one idea per section

### Layout Approach

| Element | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| **Logo** | Full-color on white; never compete with it. Give it room. |
| **Typography** | Bold, sans-serif headlines (e.g., DM Sans, Outfit, or Sora); crisp body text |
| **Photography** | Large, high-quality images of **real people** — barber chair moments, community, connection |
| **Sections** | Alternating white / light gray backgrounds for rhythm |
| **Cards** | Minimal borders, light shadows, rounded corners |
| **Buttons** | Solid red or blue; clear, single-action CTAs |

### Visual Contrast

- **Graphic logo** (busy, 3D) vs. **UI** (clean, flat)
- **Photography** (human, warm) vs. **layout** (structured, minimal)
- **Bold headlines** vs. **generous margins**

## 4. Copywriting Tone

### Voice

- **Empathetic** — "We understand. We're here."
- **Community-driven** — "together we can"
- **Hopeful** — solutions, not doom
- **Warm but professional** — nonprofit trust, not corporate polish

### Avoid

- Jargon, clinical language
- Pity or savior framing
- Over-the-top sentimentality

### Hero Headlines (Homepage)

**Primary Headline:**
> **More than a haircut—a chance to be seen.**

**Sub-headline Options:**
> Hope Cuts RI brings the barber chair to Rhode Island's shelters, recovery centers, and communities. For mental health, autism awareness, homelessness, and substance recovery—because connection changes everything.

**Alternative Headline:**
> **The chair where hope meets community.**

**Alternative Sub-headline:**
> We offer free haircuts and a listening ear across Rhode Island. From shelters to recovery centers, we meet people where they are—because together we can.

## 5. Functional Features for a Non-Profit Site

### 1. Donation Portal

- **Clear CTA** — "Donate" always visible in header
- **Simple flow** — amount selection, one-time or monthly
- **Trust signals** — secure payment (Stripe, PayPal Giving Fund), 501(c)(3) mention
- **Impact messaging** — "Your $25 helps give 3 haircuts and conversations"

### 2. Request a Visit (Shelters / Organizations)

- **Form fields:** Organization name, contact, location, population served, requested date/time window
- **Purpose:** Let shelters, recovery centers, and community orgs request Hope Cuts RI visits
- **Confirmation** — auto-reply and internal notification for follow-up

### 3. Volunteer Sign-Up

- **Fields:** Name, email, skills (barber, support, admin), availability
- **Barber vs. non-barber** — different paths for licensed barbers vs. general volunteers
- **Onboarding** — clear next steps (orientation, training) after sign-up

### 4. Impact / Stories Section

- **Stats** — haircuts given, people reached, shelters visited
- **Short stories** — anonymous or named testimonials
- **Filters** — by pillar: Autism Awareness | Mental Health | Homelessness | Substance Recovery

## 7. Key Implementation Notes

### Mobile-First Layout

Many users will be on phones—shelter residents, volunteers, donors.

- **Stack layout** — single column on mobile; multi-column on desktop
- **Touch-friendly** — 44px+ tap targets for buttons and links
- **Readable text** — 16px+ body; avoid long line lengths
- **Fast load** — optimize images; consider lazy loading

### Emphasize the Pillars

| Pillar | Placement |
|--------|-----------|
| **Respect Autism** | Our Impact, Services, hero imagery |
| **Mental Health / Depression** | Our Impact, copy, stories |
| **Homelessness** | Shelter visits, outreach, Request a Visit |
| **Substance Abuse Recovery** | Recovery centers, partnerships, impact stats |

### Logo Usage

- Use full logo on light backgrounds (white, warm gray)
- Ensure adequate padding; never crowd the logo
- Consider a simplified mark (e.g., barber pole icon) for favicon / small spaces

---

## Summary

Hope Cuts RI’s site should feel **calm, trusted, and hopeful**—using barber pole colors sparingly with neutrals—while the logo remains the bold visual anchor. Clean layout, strong typography, and real community imagery will convey "together we can" without feeling commercial or overwhelming.
